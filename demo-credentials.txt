Benchline LIMS - demo sign-in accounts (prototype only)
=======================================================

These are the seeded demo accounts. Every password is the username followed
by "-demo". They are fictional and published in the source code, so they must
never exist in a validated system.

Username      Password           Name            Role
------------  -----------------  --------------  -----------
coordinator   coordinator-demo   Morgan Lee      coordinator
analyst       analyst-demo       Alex Chen       analyst
analyst-2     analyst-2-demo     Sam Rivera      analyst
manager       manager-demo       Taylor Brooks   manager
admin         admin-demo         Jordan Park     admin

The admin account can open every view and act in every role, but it still
signs with its own password, is audit-trailed under its own name, and cannot
approve a Sample on which it entered, corrected or retested a Result
(docs/adr/0004-admin-acts-in-every-role-within-separation-of-duties.md).

These accounts are seeded from src/accounts/demo-accounts.ts (built from
`people` in src/laboratory/contracts.ts) into the database, where the Admin
manages them and adds others on the Accounts screen (ADR-0011).
