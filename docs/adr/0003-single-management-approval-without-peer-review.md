# A single Manager Approval, with no separate peer review

GMP labs commonly have a second analyst review the work before management approves it. We decided that, for now, a Sample passes through one electronically signed Approval by a Manager, who can approve it or return it to the Analyst with a comment. The lab chose to start with the simpler flow and add peer review later if needed. Three roles take part (Coordinator, Analyst, Manager), and the separation that peer review would provide is kept by one hard rule: a Manager can never approve Results they entered themselves, including their own corrections in an Amendment.

## Consequences

- The prototype's separate Reviewer role and "Review queue" are folded into the Manager's Approval.
- Adding peer review later means inserting a new signed step before Approval, not changing what Approval means.
- With no second reviewer, the Approval signature carries the second-person review of §211.194(a)(8): it records that the Results, their calculation worksheets, the original instrument data, any OOS investigations and the Sample's audit trail were reviewed (#31), and the Results tab shows the Sample's audit trail beside the Results for that review.
