# A Result that exceeds its Limit is decided only by its OOS investigation

A Result that exceeds its Limit leaves the record's current state only through its OOS investigation: the Analyst cannot correct it before or while it is investigated, and an investigation concludes Invalidated only when the Laboratory error check identified a laboratory error. We chose this over letting the Analyst correct an obvious typo with a reason, because correcting an OOS value away without an investigation is testing into compliance, the practice FDA's OOS guidance and ISO/IEC 17025 auditors look for; a typo is simply the assignable cause the investigation records.

## Consequences

- The one exception is an Amendment correcting a Confirmed OOS Result, which its investigation has already decided; the corrected value is judged afresh, and a new value over its Limit needs its own investigation.
- An investigation may still conclude Confirmed when the Laboratory error check identified an error that does not explain the value.
