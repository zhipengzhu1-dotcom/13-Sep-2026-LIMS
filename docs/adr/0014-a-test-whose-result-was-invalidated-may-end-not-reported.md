# A Test whose Result was Invalidated may end as a Not reported Test

ADR-0006 left a Test whose Result was Invalidated calling for a Retest. When the Retest maximum is used up or no retained material is left, that Sample could never be approved. We decided that the Manager may close such a Test as a Not reported Test. The Manager gives one of two reasons, _Retest maximum reached_ or _No retained material_, and cites the Nonconforming work Form, which is kept outside Benchline (ISO/IEC 17025 7.10). The Sample can then be approved, and its Certificate of Analysis says on that Test's row that it was not reported and why. We chose this over leaving the Sample unapproved under a signed notice, which was the interim bridge. A Sample that can never close reports nothing to the Client, and FDA's OOS guidance asks for "a point at which the additional testing ends".

## Consequences

- ADR-0006's "the Test calls for a Retest" now reads "unless the Manager closes it as a Not reported Test". A Retest is still the only way a new Result follows an Invalidated one. No Result is entered on a Not reported Test until the Manager reopens it with a reason, which is allowed only until the Sample's Approval. Once a Certificate version with the Test not reported is approved, no Amendment reopens it.
- Closing and reopening are not Electronic signatures, so ADR-0001's signing points are unchanged. As with concluding an investigation (ADR-0006), the audit trail attributes the close to the Manager, with the server's time, the reason and the Form reference. A reopen is a new entry with its own reason, and the close stays on record.
- Closing and reopening are not Bench work (ADR-0005). The Manager who closed the Test may give Approval. The Approval signature's review statement names any Not reported Tests and their Nonconforming work Forms, so an Approval signed after the close attests to reviewing them.
- Benchline counts no Retests (#91), so _Retest maximum reached_ rests on the Manager's attestation, and a reopen can allow Retests past the lab's maximum. The SOP and the Form are the control until Benchline counts Retests.
- The nonconforming work record and its impact on other work (ISO/IEC 17025 7.10.1 c and e) stay on the Form; Benchline keeps only its reference (attaching it is #90). The Certificate never prints the reference.
- The Certificate row lists the Test's OOS investigations with their Invalidated values and has no value, qualifier, Limit or Conformity statement. Certificate versions locked before this decision keep their rows and hashes.
