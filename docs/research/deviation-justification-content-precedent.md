## Findings

### 1. SENAITE — every deviation-adjacent text field found is free text; none is a required field; none is a structured reference to a remedial-action record

**The `Remarks` field is one shared free-text field reused across almost every content type, not a per-subject-type field.** `src/bika/lims/content/abstractbaseanalysis.py:873-879` defines it once, on the shared analysis base class:

```python
# Remarks are used in various ways by almost all objects in the system.
Remarks = TextField(
    'Remarks',
    read_permission=View,
    write_permission=FieldEditAnalysisRemarks,
    schemata='Description'
)
```

A grep for `Remarks` across `src/bika/lims/content/` and `src/senaite/core/content/` finds it declared, separately but identically shaped (`TextField`, no `required=1`), on: `abstractbaseanalysis.py` (analyses/analysis services), `analysisrequest.py` (samples), `artemplate.py`, `batch.py`, `instrumentcalibration.py:156-163`, `instrumentcertification.py:199-205`, `instrumentmaintenancetask.py`, `instrumentscheduledtask.py`, `instrumentvalidation.py`, `referencesample.py`, `supplier.py`, `worksheet.py`/`src/senaite/core/content/worksheet.py`. None of these declarations sets `required=1` or `required=True`; Archetypes `TextField` defaults to not-required. I found no `validators=(...)` clause on any of them enforcing non-blank content, and no guard in `src/bika/lims/workflow/analysis/guards.py` (`guard_reject`, `guard_retract`, `guard_verify`, `guard_multi_verify`, `guard_submit` — the full guard set) that reads or requires `Remarks` before allowing a transition. `guard_reject` (the closest analogue to a "deviation" transition) is two lines: it only checks whether dependents can also be rejected (`src/bika/lims/workflow/analysis/guards.py`, `guard_reject`). **So in SENAITE, "justified" is never enforced at the guard/schema level for any of Remarks' many uses** — an analyst can retract, reject, or leave a QC-invalid instrument's result in place without writing anything in Remarks.

**Instrument calibration and certification each carry their own free-text `Remarks`/`WorkPerformed` field, shaped identically to each other and to the analysis-level field — same `TextField`, no structured reference.** `InstrumentCalibration` additionally has a `WorkPerformed` `TextField` (`instrumentcalibration.py:112-121`, "Description of the actions made during the calibration") — this is the closest thing in SENAITE to a distinct "remedial action taken" narrative, separate from the general-purpose `Remarks` field on the same object — but it too is unstructured prose, not a reference/FK to anything, and not required.

**The one place SENAITE does combine a controlled vocabulary with free text is sample rejection (a different kind of "reason" than a per-analysis deviation) — and even there, the free-text component is optional and the two are concatenated into a single string, not kept as separate structured fields.** `RejectionWidget.getRejectionReasons()` (`src/bika/lims/browser/widgets/rejectionwidget.py:60-75`) takes a dict `{'selected': [...], 'other': '...', 'checkbox_other': True/False}` and returns `', '.join(reasons)` — the predefined multi-select options (configured lab-wide in `SenaiteSetup.getRejectionReasons()`, `src/senaite/core/content/senaitesetup.py:2118-2129`, "a list of unicode strings") plus an optional free-text "other" entry, joined into one comma-separated string when the reasons checkbox is enabled. If the checkbox is enabled but nothing is selected and "other" is blank, the method returns the literal string `"Yes, unknow"` (sic) rather than raising an error (`rejectionwidget.py:74`) — i.e., even in the one place SENAITE offers a structured vocabulary, an empty/uninformative reason is tolerated, not rejected. This mechanism is for whole-sample rejection at intake, not for a per-determination deviation at review/verification time, and its predefined `rejection_reasons` list is a **controlled vocabulary configured once for the whole lab setup** (not per deviation-subject-type, not linked to a corrective-action record).

**I found no field, anywhere in `senaite.core`, that is a foreign key/UID reference from a deviation-reason field to a corrective/remedial-action record, and no distinct "remedial action taken" reference field separate from a plain narrative — with the possible partial exception of `InstrumentCalibration.WorkPerformed` above, which is still free text, just semantically distinct from `Remarks`.**

### 2. OpenELIS Global 2 — three different shapes of "reason" field coexist in the same module, and the shapes differ by how strongly required is enforced, not by subject type

All three examples below come from the same feature area (microbiology antimicrobial-susceptibility testing, `src/main/java/org/openelisglobal/microbiology/`), not from three different "deviation subject types" in the sense the question asks about (calibration / reagent-lot / holding-time). OpenELIS Global 2 has **no instrument-calibration record at all** (confirmed independently in `docs/research/instrument-entity-lims-precedent.md` §5 — a grep for `calibrat` across `src/main/java` returns no equipment-calibration record) and **no holding-time concept** (a repository-wide case-insensitive grep for `holdingTime`, `holding_time`, `HoldingTime` across `src/main/java` and `src/main/resources` at this clone returns nothing). So the comparison below is *within* the one subject area (reagent lots and AST-panel/QC deviations) that does exist, not *across* the three subject types the ticket asks about — OpenELIS simply does not model two of those three subject types at all.

**(a) A reagent/lot pre-condition failure is a hard block with no justification field of any kind — not free text, not structured, not overridable.** `InventoryManagementServiceImpl.consumeSelectedLot()` calls `validateSelectedLot(lot, quantityNeeded)` (`src/main/java/org/openelisglobal/inventory/service/InventoryManagementServiceImpl.java:37-77`), which throws `InventoryLotUnavailableException` with a fixed code (`INVENTORY_LOT_EXPIRED`, `INVENTORY_LOT_QC_FAILED`, `INVENTORY_LOT_QC_NOT_PASSED`, or a status-derived code) if the lot is expired, QC-failed, or not `ACTIVE`/`IN_USE`. `MicroReagentLotServiceImpl.recordSelection()` (`src/main/java/org/openelisglobal/microbiology/service/MicroReagentLotServiceImpl.java:114-151`) calls this path with no reason/justification parameter anywhere in its signature, and I found no override path in the microbiology or inventory packages (`grep -rn "override|forceUse|allowExpired"` across both packages returns only the AST-QC-override machinery described in (c), which is unrelated to reagent lots). **An expired or QC-failed reagent lot cannot be used at all in this code path — there is no "recorded and justified" concept for this specific pre-condition; it is refused outright.**

**(b) An AST-panel deviation from the organism-default panel ("panel adjustment") is a plain free-text reason, conditionally required only in application code, nullable at the database level.** `MicroAstServiceImpl.startRun()` (`src/main/java/org/openelisglobal/microbiology/service/MicroAstServiceImpl.java:180-190`): if the resolved panel or antibiotic ordering differs from the organism default (`adjusted == true`), the code requires `panelAdjustmentReason` to be non-blank and throws `IllegalArgumentException("AST_PANEL_ADJUSTMENT_REASON_REQUIRED")` if not; if not adjusted, the field is simply set to `null`. The backing column, `panel_adjustment_reason`, is declared `TEXT` with **no `NOT NULL` constraint** in the schema (`src/main/resources/liquibase/3.5.x.x/072-microbiology-ast-provenance.xml:12`) — the requiredness is enforced only by the Java service method, not by the database.

**(c) A QC-failure override on an AST run is also free text, but is written into a separate, append-only, foreign-keyed event table whose `reason` column IS `NOT NULL` at the database level.** `MicroAstServiceImpl.overrideQcFailure()` (`src/main/java/org/openelisglobal/microbiology/service/MicroAstServiceImpl.java:590-608`) calls `MicroCaseServiceImpl.requireText(reason, "reason")` before proceeding, then updates `micro_ast_run.qc_override_reason` (itself a nullable `TEXT` column per `src/main/resources/liquibase/3.5.x.x/077-microbiology-ast-analyzer-lifecycle.xml:32` — application-enforced only, same shape as (b)) — **and** the individual-reading-level override mechanism writes a separate row to `micro_ast_override_event` (`src/main/java/org/openelisglobal/microbiology/valueholder/MicroAstOverrideEvent.java`), whose schema (`src/main/resources/liquibase/3.5.x.x/074-microbiology-ast-override-history.xml:9-36`) declares:
```xml
<column name="reading_id" type="VARCHAR(36)"><constraints nullable="false"/></column>
<column name="action" type="VARCHAR(20)"><constraints nullable="false"/></column>
<column name="from_interpretation" type="VARCHAR(40)"><constraints nullable="false"/></column>
<column name="to_interpretation" type="VARCHAR(40)"><constraints nullable="false"/></column>
<column name="reason" type="TEXT"><constraints nullable="false"/></column>
<column name="performed_at" type="TIMESTAMP"><constraints nullable="false"/></column>
<column name="performed_by" type="VARCHAR(20)"><constraints nullable="false"/></column>
```
plus a foreign key from `reading_id` to `micro_ast_reading.id` (`onDelete="CASCADE"`) and a `CHECK (action IN ('OVERRIDE', 'REVERT'))` constraint. **This is a database-level `NOT NULL` on the free-text reason column**, combined with structured, required, non-free-text columns alongside it (who, when, what state transition) in a dedicated append-only child table referencing the specific record the override applies to — not a single free-text field bolted onto the parent row.

**(d) A repeat/retest AST attempt's reason is enforced by a conditional database CHECK constraint, not just application code — the strongest of the three requiredness mechanisms found.** `src/main/resources/liquibase/3.5.x.x/061-microbiology-ast-attempts.xml:33-40`:
```sql
ALTER TABLE clinlims.micro_ast_run
ADD CONSTRAINT micro_ast_run_attempt_source_chk
CHECK (
    (attempt_type = 'ORIGINAL' AND source_run_id IS NULL)
    OR
    (attempt_type IN ('REPEAT', 'RETEST') AND source_run_id IS NOT NULL
        AND length(trim(attempt_reason)) > 0)
);
```
`attempt_reason` itself is a plain nullable `TEXT` column (`061-microbiology-ast-attempts.xml:14`), but the database enforces, via a `CHECK` constraint that reads a sibling column (`attempt_type`), that the free-text field cannot be blank/whitespace-only whenever the row represents a repeat or retest attempt. This is enforced independently of, and in addition to, the equivalent check in `MicroAstServiceImpl.requireAttemptReason()` (line 986-990: throws `IllegalArgumentException("AST_ATTEMPT_REASON_REQUIRED")` on a null-or-blank reason).

**So within this one module, OpenELIS has all three of: (i) a hard block with no justification field at all (reagent lot), (ii) a free-text reason required only in application code (panel adjustment, QC-override-on-the-run-row), and (iii) a free-text reason required by a database constraint (override event reason unconditionally NOT NULL in a dedicated event table; attempt reason conditionally NOT NULL via a CHECK constraint on the parent row).** In no case is the reason field a foreign key to a separate corrective-action/CAPA record or a controlled vocabulary — every "reason" found in OpenELIS is free text. The distinguishing factor between the three shapes is not the deviation subject type (all three are AST/microbiology concepts) but which specific action is being taken (consuming a lot vs. adjusting a panel vs. overriding a QC failure vs. repeating a run).

### 3. ISO/IEC 17025:2017, clause 7.10 (Nonconforming work) — specifies who must decide and that records must exist, not what the record's content or shape must be

Per the PJLA training slide deck (secondary source, see "Status of sources" above), clause 7.10.1 states the laboratory "shall have a procedure that shall be implemented when any aspect of its laboratory activities or results of this work do not conform to its own procedures or the agreed requirements of the customer (e.g. equipment or environmental conditions are out of specified limits, results of monitoring fail to meet specified criteria)." The procedure must ensure that:
- (a) responsibilities and authorities for managing nonconforming work are defined;
- (b) actions (including halting/repeating work and withholding reports) are based on risk levels the laboratory itself establishes;
- (c) an evaluation is made of the significance of the nonconforming work, including an impact analysis on previous results;
- (d) a decision is taken on the acceptability of the nonconforming work;
- (e) where necessary, the customer is notified and work is recalled;
- (f) the responsibility for authorizing resumption of work is defined.

Clause 7.10.2: "The laboratory shall retain records of nonconforming work and actions as specified in 7.10.1, bullets b) to f)." Clause 7.10.3: where the evaluation indicates the nonconforming work could recur, or there is doubt about conformity of the laboratory's own management-system operations, the laboratory "shall implement corrective action" (cross-referencing clause 8.7, Corrective action).

**Reading this against the ticket's question directly: nothing in 7.10.1–7.10.3, as transcribed in this secondary source, specifies the *content shape* of the nonconformity record — it does not say the record must be free text vs. structured, does not require a specific field for "remedial action taken" as distinct from a general narrative, and does not require a link/reference to a separate corrective-action record (7.10.3's corrective action is only triggered conditionally, by a recurrence/doubt finding, not for every nonconformity).** What it requires is that a *procedure* exist covering (a)–(f) and that *records* of items (b)–(f) be retained — i.e., it constrains what activities must occur and be evidenced (an evaluation happened, a decision was taken, authorization was defined), not the data model of the evidence. This is consistent with, and does not add detail beyond, this repository's earlier finding on the same clause in `docs/research/iso17025-record-obligations.md` ("Nonconforming work — Clause 7.10... This implies a record of the nonconformity and its disposition, though the precise record-content requirement was not independently verified against the standard's text in this pass") — the present research adds the clause's own sub-lettering (a–f) and 7.10.2/7.10.3 numbering, sourced from a different secondary document, but reaches the same substantive conclusion: **the standard is silent on record *shape*.**
