Benchline LIMS - roles, permissions and duties
===============================================

Source of truth: every rule below is enforced in the Laboratory core
(src/laboratory/handlers.ts), not just hidden in the UI. Role checks go
through requireRole / actsAs; the Admin passes every role check except the
Quality lead's, and the Quality lead is refused every command.

Roles: Coordinator, Analyst, Manager, Admin, Quality lead.
Background decisions: docs/adr/0001 (Part 11 / ISO 17025), 0003 (single
Manager Approval, no peer review), 0004 (Admin within separation of duties),
0007 (the Admin never stands in for the Quality lead).


EVERY SIGNED-IN USER
--------------------
- Sign in with their own account; the session ends on sign-out or after 15
  minutes of inactivity. Every action is attributed to the signed-in user.
- Can view all Samples, the Samples' Results and history, Certificates of
  Analysis (every version), Methods & Limits and the Audit trail.
- Can download worksheet attachments.
- Cannot edit or delete audit trail entries, Certificates, Acceptable intake
  entries or attachments. Nobody can; the application has no way to do it.


COORDINATOR
-----------
Duty: receives work into the lab, manages holds, assigns work, and issues
Certificates to the Client.

Views: Receiving, Holds, Publish queue, Samples, Methods, Audit trail.

Can:
- Receive a Submission with one or more Samples (full receipt record).
  Correcting the received date/time requires a reason.
- Record a Receipt deviation on a Received Sample, which puts it On hold.
- Record the Client's instruction for a held Sample (contact and time
  required): proceed -> Received, withdraw -> Withdrawn.
- Assign or reassign a Sample to an Analyst (or the Admin). Not while the
  Sample is On hold or Withdrawn.
- Record that the Client was informed of a Confirmed OOS (contact, date and
  time). Required before publishing that Sample.
- Publish an Approved Sample's Certificate: recipient(s), channel (email,
  portal, print, other) and time. The system does not send it.
- Start an Amendment on a Published Sample, with a reason.

Cannot: enter Results, give Approval, return Samples, run OOS investigations,
or add Acceptable intake entries.


ANALYST
-------
Duty: performs the testing on Samples assigned to them and records Results.

Views: My work, Methods, Audit trail.

Can (only on a Sample assigned to them):
- Upload calculation worksheets (PDF, XLSX, XLS, CSV, PNG, JPEG; 10 MB max).
- Enter a Result per Test: value in the reporting unit, instrument reading,
  sequence ID and worksheet. Qualifier (< LOD / < LOQ), Limit and Conformity
  statement are assigned by the system.
- Correct a Result, with a reason. Earlier Results stay in history.
  Results can be entered or corrected only while the Sample is In progress,
  Returned or Amendment in progress, and never while it is On hold/Withdrawn.
- Submit for Approval once every Test has a current Result. This is an
  Electronic signature (own password): "performed by", or "amended by" when
  submitting an Amendment.
- Record the laboratory error check on an open OOS investigation.

Cannot: work on Samples assigned to someone else, receive or assign Samples,
give Approval, open or conclude OOS investigations, or publish.


MANAGER
-------
Duty: reviews and approves the work, and runs OOS investigations.

Views: Approval queue, OOS investigations, Methods, Audit trail.

Can:
- Give Approval to a Sample Awaiting approval. This is an Electronic signature
  (own password): "approved by". Approval locks the Results and produces the
  next Certificate of Analysis version.
  Approval is refused while any OOS investigation is open, while a
  "Does not comply" Result has no closed investigation, or while an
  Invalidated result has no retest.
- Return a Sample Awaiting approval to its Analyst, with a comment.
- Open an OOS investigation on a "Does not comply" Result.
- Conclude an OOS investigation (after the Analyst's lab error check) as
  Invalidated (assignable cause required; a retest is then needed) or
  Confirmed, which closes it.
- Start an Amendment on a Published Sample, with a reason.
- Add a new Acceptable intake entry (append-only; no screen in this release).

Cannot: approve a Sample on which they entered, corrected or retested a
Result (separation of duties). Cannot receive, assign, enter Results, record
Client notifications or publish.


ADMIN
-----
Duty: covers any job in a small lab, so one person can stand in for any role.

Views: every view but the Quality lead's Invalid signatures (Receiving, Holds,
Samples, Publish queue, My work, Approval queue, OOS investigations, Methods,
Audit trail).

Can: everything the Coordinator, Analyst and Manager can do, including being
assigned Samples and entering Results on them. Never acts as the Quality
lead, whose review covers the Admin's own work.

Still bound by:
- Own password for every Electronic signature, recorded under the Admin's own
  printed name; every action is audit-trailed as the Admin.
- Separation of duties: cannot approve a Sample on which the Admin entered,
  corrected or retested a Result.
- Results only on Samples assigned to the Admin.
- Accounts screen (before Go-live only, ADR-0011): add users, change a
  printed name or role, deactivate or reactivate, set a password and unlock a
  Locked account. Every change is an Access event in the audit trail; no
  password is shown or recorded. Usernames are never reused, and Benchline
  always keeps at least one active Admin.


QUALITY LEAD
------------
Duty: reviews the laboratory record for the quality system: the lab-wide
audit trail (Acceptable intakes, attachments, Access events) and every
Sample with an invalid Electronic signature, which is investigated as
nonconforming work under the SOP.

Views: Samples, Invalid signatures, Methods, Audit trail.

Can: see every Sample, its Results, history, signatures and Certificates;
the lab-wide audit trail; and the Invalid signatures list, every Sample with
a signature that no longer matches its Signed content, each marked
"Signature invalid", in any state.

Cannot: run any command, in any state of the record: no receipt, assignment,
Result, worksheet upload, Electronic signature, OOS work, publication,
Amendment or Acceptable intake entry. The Admin never stands in for the
Quality lead; the SOP names a deputy with a Quality lead account of their
own instead, who also approves any change to the Quality lead's account.


LOCKED STATES (apply to everyone, including the Admin)
------------------------------------------------------
- Approved:  only Publish and the Client OOS notification are allowed.
- Published: only Start Amendment is allowed.
- Withdrawn: nothing is allowed.
- On hold:   no assignment, Results or OOS investigation until the Client's
             instruction is recorded.
